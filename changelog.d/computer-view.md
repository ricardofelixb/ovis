### Added
- A Computer button next to Chat and Terminal shows the PC's desktop, live and with its sound, over
  the pane, which keeps running under it. It runs screen-here's bridge, named by
  `OVIS_SCREEN_BRIDGE`, to the pane's PC (this PC's own desktop directly, another over SSH), and
  passes it on behind this server's sign-in. A watching device does not get it.
- On a touch screen the Computer view works like a laptop's trackpad, and the Terminal's key bar
  sits under it: its keys, held Ctrl, Alt and Shift, and a keyboard button whose typing goes to
  the desktop. A corner button shows the desktop full screen.
- The Computer view stays connected, out of sight and without sound, for 2 minutes after Chat or
  Terminal is picked, so coming back to it is instant.
