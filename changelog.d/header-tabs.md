### Changed
- The tabs now sit in the header in place of the agent's title, which frees a row on phones and
  desktops. Each tab shows its agents' friends instead of a status dot (two faces on a split), and
  an unnamed tab shows its agent's title. A tab's ⋯ menu names its workspace and branch.
- One button at the end of the tabs shows the open pane's view and opens Chat, Terminal and
  Computer. Computer is now remembered per pane, like Chat and Terminal.
- Agents under a workspace in the sidebar are indented without tree lines; only worktrees keep
  them.
- The context circle leads the model pill, in place of the model maker's logo.
