### Changed
- The tabs now sit in the header in place of the agent's title, which frees a row on phones and
  desktops. Each tab shows its agents' friends instead of a status dot (two faces on a split), and
  an unnamed tab shows its agent's title. A tab's ⋯ menu names its workspace and branch.
- One button at the end of the tabs shows the open pane's view and opens Chat and Terminal.
- A PC's desktop opens from the monitor button in its sidebar header, over the whole pane area,
  instead of from each pane's view switch. Picking an agent or a tab closes it.
- Agents under a workspace in the sidebar are indented without tree lines; only worktrees keep
  them. A selected row is marked by a slightly stronger fill alone, without the leading stroke.
- The context circle leads the model pill, in place of the model maker's logo.

### Fixed
- The + no longer shows the empty PC page for a moment before the new tab opens.
