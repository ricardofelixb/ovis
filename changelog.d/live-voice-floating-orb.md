### Changed
- A live voice call is now a small floating orb; a tap on it slides out Mute and End. Drag it
  anywhere: it snaps to the nearer side and stays there on that device.
- The call follows the selected agent: switching ends it, and after a second on another Pi a new
  call starts there with the chat so far. The message box's button no longer hangs up, so Stop
  shows during a call.

### Removed
- The full-screen call and its end-of-call screen; why a call stopped now shows beside the orb.
